abstract: |-
  Focusing on Caizi Lake during the initial stage of the Yangtze-to-Huaihe Water Diversion Project, this study evaluates how hydrological connectivity affects zooplankton diversity, environmental drivers, and community assembly. The results indicate reduced diversity during disturbance, shifts in key environmental controls, stronger homogenizing dispersal during peak disturbance, and increasing heterogeneous selection during recovery.

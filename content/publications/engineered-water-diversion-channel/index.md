abstract: |-
  This study examines how an engineered water diversion channel differs from a connected receiving lake in zooplankton community structure, assembly processes, and co-occurrence network robustness. The results highlight stronger homogenizing dispersal, a shift toward small-bodied rotifers, and reduced network robustness in the channel, with implications for ecological risk assessment and adaptive management of inter-basin water diversion systems.

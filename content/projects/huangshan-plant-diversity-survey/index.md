**Survey Period:** April 2024 – March 2026  
**Role:** Field Project Lead

This project focused on documenting plant diversity in Huangshan, Anhui, China.

As the Field Project Lead, I was responsible for the overall coordination and implementation of the project, including organizing and conducting field surveys, collecting and curating project data, and preparing project reports.

**Follow-on Publication:** As of September 2026, an illustrated guide to the common plants of Huangshan, led by Professor Zhongze Zhou, is in preparation. The first draft has been completed, and revision and publication work are ongoing.

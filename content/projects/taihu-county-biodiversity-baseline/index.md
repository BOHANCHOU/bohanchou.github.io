**Period:** March 2024 – September 2025  
**Role:** On-site Project Lead

This commissioned project conducted a comprehensive biodiversity baseline survey in Taihu County, Anqing, Anhui, China.

As the On-site Project Lead, I had primary responsibility for the plant survey component, including field survey organization, plant and vegetation investigations, and the collection and organization of field records and project data. I also made major contributions to the preparation and writing of the final project report and coordinated project-wide biodiversity outreach and science communication activities.

**Period:** March 2024 – September 2025  
**Role:** Plant Survey Lead

This commissioned project conducted a comprehensive biodiversity baseline survey in Taihu County, Anqing, Anhui, China.

As the Plant Survey Lead, I coordinated the operational implementation of the plant survey component, including field survey organization, plant and vegetation investigations, and the collection and organization of field records and project data. I also contributed to the preparation and writing of project reports and was responsible for coordinating project-wide biodiversity outreach and science communication activities.
